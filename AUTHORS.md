## Author Biography

Samir Baladi is an interdisciplinary researcher working at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, computational linguistics, and AI-driven solutions for under-resourced languages. He is affiliated with the Ronin Institute and the Rite of Renaissance research program.

**moroccan_nlp** is part of the GITDEEPER LAB ZERO V6 series, applying open-source principles to develop linguistic resources and NLP models for Moroccan Darija and Arabic. The project builds on DarijaBERT, the first BERT model for the Moroccan Arabic dialect, and extends its capabilities to new tasks and applications.
